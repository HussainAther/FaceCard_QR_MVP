# Store listing draft

## Name

FaceCard QR

## Subtitle / short description

Private, intentional contact exchange

## Promotional text

Create a personal QR card, choose every detail you share, and exchange contacts in person—without a public profile or social feed.

## Full description

FaceCard QR turns intentional, in-person introductions into a clean private contact exchange.

Create a personal visual identity from photos you explicitly select. Processing happens locally on your device. Your photos and local visual descriptor are never placed in the QR. Then build a card and choose each detail you want to share: name, email, phone, website, and more with FaceCard Pro.

Show your QR to someone nearby. They receive only the fields you enabled and can save them directly to Contacts.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN

- No public profile
- No feed, followers, likes, or discovery
- No stranger identification or face search
- No cloud biometric database
- No hidden contact fields in the QR
- Delete your identity and local data at any time

FACECARD PRO — ONE-TIME PURCHASE

Create Work, Personal, Conference, and Emergency cards; unlock extra fields and themes; export high-resolution images and PDFs; and back up non-biometric card configuration.

The generated visual identifier is not guaranteed to be globally unique and is not proof of legal identity or an authentication credential.

## Review notes

Face processing is restricted to 3–5 user-selected self-enrollment photos. There is no feature for photographing or identifying another person. To test Pro, use the attached non-consumable promo code and then test Restore Purchase in Settings. Camera is used for QR scanning and optional enrollment selfies; Contacts is requested only after Save Contact; photo access is requested for explicit selection/export actions.
