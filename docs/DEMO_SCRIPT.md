# Two-minute Shipaton demo script

Target: 1:48–1:55. Record on real devices. Avoid third-party marks/music and do not show real private contact details.

| Time | Picture | Voiceover |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | Welcome screen, then privacy screen | “FaceCard QR is identity without profile performance: an intentional contact exchange with no account, feed, discovery, or biometric cloud.” |
| 0:10–0:28 | Add three staged selfies; show three checks | “I enroll only myself with photos I explicitly select. Face detection, landmarks, averaging, and SHA-256 identity generation run locally. Photos and the visual descriptor never enter the QR.” |
| 0:28–0:38 | Identity progress, then editor | “The app creates a personal visual identity—not a claim of global uniqueness.” |
| 0:38–0:58 | Enter demo name/title/email/site; toggle phone off, email/site/GitHub on | “Every field has an independent control. If I turn phone off, it is physically absent from the payload.” |
| 0:58–1:10 | Show finished card and real QR | “The result is deliberately photo-light and reliably scannable.” |
| 1:10–1:28 | Second phone scans; received screen shows only selected fields | “The receiving phone validates the QR as untrusted input and displays only what I chose. It can save a contact, open the site, or copy the email.” |
| 1:28–1:43 | Attempt alternate card; show Pro paywall; complete sandbox purchase | “A one-time FaceCard Pro purchase through RevenueCat unlocks multiple contexts, more fields, themes, and high-resolution exports—without turning privacy into a subscription.” |
| 1:43–1:51 | Settings: restore purchase, configuration export | “Purchases restore cleanly, and non-biometric card configuration can be backed up.” |
| 1:51–1:57 | Delete identity confirmation | “The user can permanently delete the enrolled identity and local cards at any time.” |
| 1:57–2:00 | QR card hero | “FaceCard QR: share contact, not a public persona.” |

## Recording checklist

- Use two physical phones and sandbox/test purchase accounts.
- Use fictional `demo@facecard.app`, not personal information.
- Zoom enough for judges to read field switches and received values.
- Show the store purchase sheet or approved RevenueCat sandbox flow on-device.
- Export 1080p; public YouTube/Vimeo; verify duration under 2:00.
